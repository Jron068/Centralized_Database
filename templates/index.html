How to get here

The Pansol Resorts is located at Baranggay Pansol, Calamba, Philippines, 3 hours drive from Metro Manila.

By Car: Take SLEX → CALAX → Exit to Pansol

By Bus: Ride a bus bound for Los Baños or Calamba and ask to be dropped off at Pansol
